# One Spend — feedback & issues

One Spend is a simple, private subscription tracker for Android. It shows what you spend each month and reminds you before every renewal.

This repository is where testers and users report bugs and suggest features. The app's source code isn't here.

**[Report a bug](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)** · **[Suggest a feature](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)** · **[Privacy policy](https://syedarhamraza.github.io/privacy/)**

## Join the beta

One Spend is in closed testing on Google Play.

1. Open the opt-in page with the Google account you use in the Play Store: <https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.syedarhamraza.onespend>
2. Tap **Become a tester**.
3. Tap **download it on Google Play** and install the app as usual.

If the page says the app isn't available, check that the Play Store is signed in with the same Google account. If you installed an APK outside the Play Store, uninstall it first.

## Reporting a bug

1. Search the [open issues](../../issues) first. If someone has already reported it, add a 👍 or a comment with your phone model.
2. Otherwise [open a bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). You'll find the app version under **Settings › About One Spend**.
3. Screenshots and screen recordings help a lot.

No GitHub account? Use **Settings › Help & feedback › Report a problem** in the app instead. It fills in your app version and phone model.

## Keep your details private

Issues here are **public**. Don't post card numbers, bank details, email addresses or anything else personal. Before you share a screenshot or a backup file, blur or remove anything private. Subscription names and prices are fine only if you don't mind other people seeing them.

## What happens next

Each issue gets a label. `needs-info` means I need more details from you. An issue is closed when it's fixed in a release, or when it won't be fixed, with a reason. Fixes are listed in the app under **Settings › About One Spend › What's new**.
